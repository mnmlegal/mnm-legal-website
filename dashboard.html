Confidentiality & Data Protection

This workspace is intended exclusively for authorised professional use. Staff members must maintain the confidentiality of client information and office records.

Staff authorization is checked through Firebase Authentication and Firestore. Existing invoices remain stored in the original browser.

Local invoice records are not encrypted or protected by Firestore Security Rules. Cloud invoice storage is not yet enabled.

Do not clear browser data without preserving your existing invoice records. Always sign out after using a shared computer.
