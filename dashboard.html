Confidentiality & Data Protection

This workspace is intended exclusively for authorised professional use. Staff members must maintain the confidentiality of client information and office records.

Staff authorization is checked through Firebase Authentication and Firestore staff profiles.

Client records and professional invoices are managed through centralised Firebase Cloud Firestore storage, subject to the applicable database security rules.

Access to Client Management and Invoice Generator is currently restricted to authorised administrators.

Maintain periodic independent backups of important office records. Store exported files securely and restrict access to authorised persons.

Always sign out after using a shared computer. Never disclose your account credentials to unauthorised persons.
