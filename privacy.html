Our Approach to Privacy

MNM Legal Associates, hereinafter referred to as the "Firm", "we", "us" or "our", recognises the importance of privacy, professional confidentiality and responsible handling of personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, received, used, stored, disclosed and otherwise processed in connection with our website, professional communications and legal services.

This Policy shall be read subject to applicable Indian law, professional obligations and any specific terms governing an individual professional engagement.

Privacy

Responsible handling of personal information for identified purposes.

Professional Confidentiality

Respect for applicable professional and statutory confidentiality obligations.

Information Security

Reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information.

Transparency

Information about relevant processing purposes and privacy-related requests.

SECTION 01

Scope and Application

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed in connection with:

  • The official website of the Firm, https://mnmlegals.com/.
  • Website contact forms and enquiries.
  • Telephone, email and other electronic communications with the Firm.
  • Professional consultations and legal service engagements.
  • Legal documentation, correspondence, administrative and billing activities.
  • Other lawful professional activities undertaken by the Firm.

Third-party websites and independently operated platforms may be governed by their respective privacy policies.

SECTION 02

Information We May Collect

The nature and extent of information collected depend upon the purpose of the interaction and the relevant legal matter.

2.1 Personal and Contact Information

  • Name and contact particulars.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Residential or correspondence address.
  • Professional and organisational particulars.
  • Identity particulars, where necessary.

2.2 Legal Matter Information

  • Facts and circumstances of legal disputes.
  • Relevant legal and contractual documents.
  • Judicial and administrative records.
  • Correspondence and supporting evidence.
  • Information concerning relevant parties.
  • Instructions received in professional engagements.

2.3 Financial and Billing Information

  • Professional fee and invoice particulars.
  • Payment references and transaction records.
  • Tax-related particulars, where applicable.
  • Information necessary for accounting and compliance.

2.4 Technical Information

Website hosting providers and other technical service providers may process information such as IP addresses, browser details, device information, access times and technical request logs in connection with website delivery and security.

SECTION 03

Methods of Information Collection

Information may be received through:

  • Website enquiry and contact forms.
  • Direct email and telephone communications.
  • WhatsApp or other communication platforms.
  • Personal or online consultations.
  • Documents provided by clients or authorised representatives.
  • Publicly accessible judicial and government records.
  • Lawful communications from relevant third parties.
  • Technical systems used for website operation.

Individuals providing information concerning another person should ensure that such disclosure is lawful and appropriately authorised, where authorisation is required.

SECTION 04

Purpose of Information Processing

Personal information may be processed for legitimate professional, administrative and legally permissible purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and communications.
  • Assessing requests for professional assistance.
  • Conducting conflict-of-interest checks.
  • Providing legal advice where professionally engaged.
  • Preparing legal documents and correspondence.
  • Representing clients before competent forums.
  • Maintaining professional and case-related records.
  • Managing appointments and consultations.
  • Preparing invoices and maintaining payment records.
  • Complying with statutory and professional obligations.
  • Maintaining website functionality and security.
  • Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

Information shall not knowingly be processed for an unrelated purpose without an applicable lawful basis.

SECTION 05

Legal Basis and Consent

Personal information may be processed on the basis of consent or another lawful ground recognised under applicable Indian law.

Where consent is required, the relevant purpose of processing shall be communicated in accordance with applicable requirements.

Merely accessing or browsing this website shall not, by itself, constitute consent to every form of personal data processing.

Where processing is based on consent, an individual may withdraw such consent through the applicable communication channel, subject to statutory requirements and the consequences of withdrawal.

Withdrawal of consent does not ordinarily affect the lawfulness of processing undertaken before such withdrawal.

SECTION 06

Client Confidentiality and Professional Communications

The Firm recognises the professional obligations of advocates concerning confidential client communications and information received during professional engagements.

Professional communications shall be handled subject to applicable legal protections and professional obligations, including, where applicable:

  • The Advocates Act, 1961.
  • Applicable Bar Council of India Rules.
  • Sections 132 to 134 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.
  • Other applicable statutory and professional confidentiality requirements.

Professional confidentiality and evidentiary privilege are subject to their respective legal conditions, limitations and statutory exceptions.

Nothing in this Policy shall be interpreted as authorising disclosure of privileged communications contrary to applicable law.

Confidential Communications

Please avoid submitting confidential evidence, privileged documents or detailed sensitive information through a general website enquiry form. Appropriate communication arrangements may be discussed directly with the Firm.

SECTION 07

Advocate-Client Relationship

Merely visiting this website, reading published legal information, using an interactive tool or submitting a general enquiry does not automatically establish an advocate-client relationship.

Acceptance of a professional engagement may depend upon conflict checks, the nature of the matter, mutual understanding and applicable professional requirements.

Whether an advocate-client relationship or a legally protected professional communication exists shall ultimately depend on the facts and applicable law, rather than this Policy alone.

Visitors should not assume that submitting information through a public website form amounts to acceptance of instructions or undertaking of responsibility for a legal matter.

SECTION 08

Sensitive and Confidential Information

Certain legal matters may involve information requiring heightened confidentiality or security considerations, including:

  • Identity and financial records.
  • Medical and health-related information.
  • Matrimonial and family-related information.
  • Information concerning children.
  • Criminal proceedings and allegations.
  • Personal photographs and recordings.
  • Confidential commercial information.
  • Property and inheritance-related documents.

Such information should be provided only where relevant and necessary for the particular professional purpose.

The Firm seeks to handle such information with safeguards appropriate to its nature and applicable legal obligations.

Information Security Advisory

Do not submit passwords, banking PINs, one-time passwords, card security codes or unnecessary identity document numbers through the website contact form.

SECTION 09

Disclosure and Sharing of Information

Personal information may be disclosed where necessary and legally permissible, subject to applicable confidentiality and professional obligations.

Relevant recipients may include:

  • Advocates, authorised personnel and professional associates involved in a matter.
  • Courts, tribunals and competent statutory or administrative authorities.
  • Experts, translators, consultants and other necessary professional service providers.
  • Opposing parties or their representatives, where required for lawful legal proceedings.
  • Technology, communication and administrative service providers.
  • Other persons where disclosure is authorised or required by applicable law.

The Firm's policy is not to sell personal information or knowingly disclose confidential client information for unrelated advertising purposes.

Information filed before courts or other authorities may become part of judicial or public records, subject to applicable confidentiality, sealing and access restrictions.

SECTION 10

The website and professional communications may rely upon independently operated technology services.

10.1 Website Hosting

The website hosting infrastructure may process technical request information necessary for website delivery, operation and security.

10.2 Contact Form Processing

The website contact form may use FormSubmit to transmit enquiry information to the Firm's designated email address.

Information entered into such a form may be processed by FormSubmit and the relevant email service providers before reaching the Firm.

10.5 Communication and Social Media Platforms

Communications or interactions through WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, X and other external platforms are also subject to the respective platform's policies and terms of service.

This Policy does not replace the privacy policies of independent third-party service providers.

SECTION 11

Cookies and Browser Storage

Websites may use cookies, session storage, local storage and similar browser-based technologies for different functional purposes.

11.1 Essential Website Functionality

The website may use browser session storage to remember whether a visitor has acknowledged the website's legal disclaimer during the relevant browsing session.

Acknowledgement of the legal disclaimer is not equivalent to consent to unrelated personal data processing or tracking.

11.2 Restricted Administrative Utilities

Internal office utilities may use browser storage to maintain application preferences, invoice records and related administrative data.

Such browser storage is separate from ordinary public website browsing.

11.3 Third-Party Technologies

Embedded resources and external service providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies, depending upon the service and browser configuration.

11.4 Browser Controls

Visitors may manage cookies and website storage through their browser settings. Restricting certain technologies may affect website functionality.

Administrative Data Warning

Clearing browser or website storage on an office device may permanently remove locally stored administrative records, including invoices, where no independent backup exists. Authorised personnel should preserve appropriate backups before clearing such storage.

SECTION 12

Interactive Legal Tools and Calculators

The website may contain legal calculators, document finders, preliminary checklists and other informational utilities.

These tools are intended to provide general educational and informational references.

Where a tool operates entirely within the visitor's browser, the values entered into that tool are not intentionally transmitted to the Firm by the tool itself.

This does not exclude ordinary technical processing associated with website hosting or external resources.

Visitors should not enter confidential personal identifiers or sensitive case information into public legal utilities.

Results generated by these tools do not constitute a legal opinion, determination of rights or acceptance of professional instructions.

SECTION 13

Information Security

The Firm seeks to maintain reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of information processed and applicable legal requirements.

Relevant safeguards may include, where implemented and appropriate:

  • Access restrictions for professional records.
  • Appropriate account and device security.
  • Controlled sharing of confidential documents.
  • Secure communication arrangements.
  • Record preservation and backup procedures.
  • Review of relevant security risks.

No internet-based communication or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Where a personal data breach occurs, applicable statutory obligations concerning assessment, response and notification shall be followed.

This provision does not exclude or restrict any mandatory responsibility imposed by applicable law.

SECTION 14

Data Retention and Preservation

Personal information may be retained for such period as is reasonably necessary for the relevant processing purpose or as required or permitted by applicable law.

Relevant retention considerations may include:

  • Duration of the professional engagement.
  • Pending judicial or administrative proceedings.
  • Applicable statutory limitation periods.
  • Professional record preservation requirements.
  • Accounting, taxation and audit obligations.
  • Establishment or defence of legal claims.
  • Applicable legal holds and preservation directions.

Information that is no longer required shall be considered for appropriate deletion, disposal or anonymisation, subject to applicable legal and professional obligations.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove information retained in lawful backup systems or records maintained by independent authorities.

SECTION 15

Individual Privacy and Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law, commencement of relevant statutory provisions and legally recognised exceptions, individuals may have rights concerning their personal data.

These may include:

  • Access to prescribed information concerning personal data processing.
  • Correction of inaccurate or misleading personal information.
  • Completion or updating of relevant personal information.
  • Erasure of personal data where applicable legal conditions are satisfied.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing is based upon consent.
  • Access to applicable grievance redressal mechanisms.
  • Nomination of another person to exercise applicable statutory rights in the event of death or incapacity, where provided by law.

Appropriate verification of identity or representative authority may be required before processing a request.

Requests may be subject to lawful restrictions, including professional confidentiality, third-party rights, statutory record preservation and pending legal proceedings.

Privacy-related requests may be sent to mnmlegal.in@gmail.com .

SECTION 16

Children's Privacy

The public website is not specifically directed towards children.

Information concerning children may, however, be relevant in legal matters involving guardianship, custody, maintenance, inheritance, protection and other proceedings.

Processing of such information shall be subject to applicable statutory requirements, lawful authority, relevant professional obligations and the interests of the child.

Where legally required, appropriate parental or lawful guardian consent shall be obtained, subject to applicable statutory exceptions.

The Firm's policy is not to knowingly undertake targeted advertising directed at children using their personal data.

SECTION 17

International Processing and Data Transfers

Certain hosting, email, communication and technology service providers may operate infrastructure outside India.

Accordingly, information processed through such services may be stored or otherwise processed in jurisdictions outside India.

Such processing shall remain subject to applicable Indian legal requirements, relevant restrictions on cross-border data transfers and professional confidentiality obligations.

SECTION 18

External Websites and Government Portals

The website may contain links to government portals, judicial websites, statutory authorities, legal information resources and other external websites.

These websites are independently operated and may have their own information collection practices, privacy policies and security arrangements.

Visitors should review the relevant policies before submitting personal information through external websites.

The Firm does not control the independent processing activities of such websites.

SECTION 19

Professional and Administrative Communications

Contact information may be used to communicate concerning:

  • Enquiries and requested consultations.
  • Professional engagements.
  • Case-related developments and instructions.
  • Appointment arrangements.
  • Professional fees and invoices.
  • Administrative and statutory matters.
  • Privacy-related requests and responses.

Communications shall remain subject to applicable professional conduct rules and statutory requirements.

Individuals may request discontinuation of optional communications, subject to communications necessary for existing engagements or legal obligations.

SECTION 20

Privacy Grievances and Redressal

Individuals having questions, concerns or grievances regarding personal information may communicate with the Firm through its designated privacy contact.

A request should ordinarily contain:

  • Name and appropriate contact particulars.
  • Description of the privacy concern.
  • Relevant processing activity or communication.
  • Nature of the clarification or remedy sought.
  • Relevant supporting information, where necessary.

The Firm shall endeavour to address privacy-related grievances within the period prescribed by applicable law or, where no specific period applies, within a reasonable period.

Nothing in this Policy restricts the right of an individual to approach a competent statutory authority or other legally available forum, subject to applicable procedural requirements.

SECTION 21

Applicable Legal Framework

This Privacy Policy shall be interpreted in accordance with applicable laws of India, including, to the extent relevant and in force:

  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules and notifications.
  • The Information Technology Act, 2000.
  • Applicable rules concerning personal information and information security, insofar as they remain in force.
  • The Advocates Act, 1961.
  • The Bar Council of India Rules.
  • The Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.
  • Other applicable statutory, regulatory and professional requirements.

References to statutory rights and obligations are subject to the commencement, applicability, amendments and exemptions prescribed under the relevant legislation.

Any dispute concerning this Policy shall be subject to the jurisdiction of legally competent courts, tribunals or statutory authorities.

Nothing in this Policy shall exclude mandatory statutory rights or confer jurisdiction upon a forum that otherwise lacks jurisdiction under applicable law.

SECTION 22

Amendments to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised from time to time to reflect changes in applicable law, professional practices, website functionality, technology services or information processing arrangements.

The revised version shall be published on this page with an appropriate revision date.

Where applicable law requires separate notice or fresh consent for a material change in processing, the relevant requirement shall be complied with.

Continued use of the website shall not automatically constitute consent to materially different processing where separate consent is legally required.
